# Mohammadmostafa Rostamkhani

I work on natural language processing and multimodal learning, with an interest in how language and vision-language models reason and respond to challenging inputs. I completed my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST) in November 2024. I currently work as an NLP Researcher at ZharfaTech.

[Website](https://mohammadmostafarostamkhani.github.io/) · [CV](https://mohammadmostafarostamkhani.github.io/assets/cv/Mohammadmostafa_Rostamkhani_CV.pdf) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oDcx--cAAAAJ&hl=en) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Mohammadmostafa)

## Research interests

- Multimodal learning, vision-language reasoning, and visual question answering.
- Natural language processing, including multilingual learning, claim retrieval, and text classification.
- Evaluation of language and vision-language models on reasoning and perceptual challenges.
